Entry for the [One-Liner Laughs](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-liner-laughs-aa872d41) bounty on make.fun: a single funny line of at most 200 characters. `main.py` writes the line — and nothing else — to `/output/entry.txt`.

The line was picked by the winners' method: 8 drafts written, then duel-tested pairwise in both orders against the judge's own criteria (real one-line joke, laugh-out-loud, surprise twist, freshness, economy of wording). The duel winner, 14 wins out of 14 games:

> The hotel charged me $18 for a “view of the sea” — it was a postcard taped to the window, and the sea was photoshopped.

(119 characters.)

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
